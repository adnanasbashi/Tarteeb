TRUST

Return & customer policy

Returns

For stocked products, customers should contact us within the published return window after delivery if the item is damaged, defective or materially different from the description. The final return window, exclusions and refund/replacement process must be confirmed before launch according to applicable Lebanese requirements and courier/payment procedures.

Special orders

Because special-order products are purchased specifically for the customer, cancellation/return eligibility may differ. We disclose the terms before the customer approves the order.

Damaged on arrival

Customers should photograph the package and product and contact support promptly. We will review the case and coordinate replacement/refund according to the final policy.

Pickup

Pickup orders are considered fulfilled when collected after customer confirmation. Uncollected orders may be subject to a storage/return rule shown at checkout.

Privacy

Customer contact information should only be used for order fulfillment, support and opted-in marketing. The production version should include a proper privacy notice and secure backend storage.

Launch note: This storefront is ready for pilot testing. Before production orders, connect a secure backend/database, replace demo products/photos, confirm courier dates, legal policies and payment integrations.
